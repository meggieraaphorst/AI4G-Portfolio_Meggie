**Project title:**
Hidden cost

**My pair partner:**
Benjamin

**Tool we had to use:**
ComfyUI

**SDG we had to address:**
SDG 13 – Climate Action

**What problem does it solve, and for whom?**
_Fast fashion can make clothing feel cheap and disposable, while the environmental impact behind the production of a T-shirt is not visible when someone buys it.

Our project focuses on young adults aged 16–25 who regularly buy affordable or trendy fast-fashion clothing. They see the product and its price in the store, but not the production process and environmental impact behind it.

The project is not specifically aimed at people who already actively avoid fast fashion or professionals who already have extensive knowledge about the textile industry._

**What did you build?**
_We created an AI-generated short film called **Hidden Cost**.

The film follows the journey of a T-shirt. It shows cotton, large-scale textile production, energy use and emissions, a fast-fashion store, the T-shirt being bought and worn, and finally clothing waste.

The final message is:

**“The price tag doesn't show the full cost.”**_

The purpose of the film is to make an impact that is normally hidden from the consumer more visible and encourage the target audience to think about their clothing choices.

**Ethical reflection - what are the risks of your tool? Who could it harm?**
_One risk of our film is that the AI-generated scenes look realistic even though they do not show real factories, people or events. A viewer could incorrectly interpret the images as documentary footage. To reduce this risk, we clearly describe the film as AI-generated and do not claim that any of the locations or people shown are real.

Another risk is oversimplification. The environmental impact of clothing production is complex, while our short film tells the story in only a few scenes. For example, the factory and emissions scenes are visual representations of the environmental impact of production, not documentation of one specific factory. We therefore avoid presenting the generated scenes as factual evidence of a particular company, location or event.

AI image and video models can also reproduce stereotypes or biases from their training data. We tried to reduce unwanted results by writing specific positive and negative prompts and checking the generated output before including it in the final film.

If we continued the project, we would further validate the environmental claims used in the film with reliable sources and test the video with people from our target audience to see whether the message is understood as intended._

## 4. Reflection

**What is the most important thing I learned this week?**
The most important thing I learned this week is that AI video generation takes a lot of experimenting and adjusting. At first, we wanted to generate images in ComfyUI and use those images to create the videos. However, the image-to-video results were often strange and not what we wanted. Because of this, we switched to text-to-video. This worked better, but we still had to change our prompts and generate some scenes multiple times. I learned that with AI, your first idea or result does not always work, and sometimes you have to change your approach.
I also learned much more about ComfyUI. In the beginning, I found the nodes and workflows quite confusing, especially when we started working with video. By working on the project, I started to understand better how the different nodes connect and how you can use them to generate clips and combine everything into one final video.

**Where does this connect to "AI for Good"?**
_This project connects to AI for Good because we used AI to create awareness about the environmental impact of fast fashion and its connection to SDG 13, Climate Action. With Hidden Cost, we wanted to show young people that there is more behind a cheap T-shirt than just the price they see in the store.
At the same time, I learned that it is important to be careful with AI-generated visuals. The scenes can look realistic even though the people, factories and situations are not real. That is why we should be transparent that the film is AI-generated and not present the generated scenes as real footage. This made me realize that using AI for a good cause also means thinking about how people might interpret what you create._
